# Promo storylines

Three candidate films for the my-ui promo: 90 s, lively and fast-cut, music and effects, no
voiceover. The demos' jokes, avatars and photos carry the energy. Chinese on-screen copy.

## A. 评论区开会

- **Form**: the comment section is the narrator. Every scene is introduced by a meme user's comment.
- **Sees and feels**: a feed of jokes that keeps scrolling into the four UIs.
- **First two seconds**: one comment bubble, 「老登本登：年轻人搞这么多皮肤干嘛」, pops on paper.
- **Weakest point**: the UIs become a backdrop for the jokes.

## B. 柒夜的一天

- **Form**: a day in the life; each UI is one part of 柒夜's day abroad.
- **Sees and feels**: morning forum, afternoon blog, evening gossip, midnight terminal.
- **First two seconds**: the cat-eared avatar waking up next to an alarm clock.
- **Weakest point**: slow to show what my-ui is.

## C. my-ui (picked, see `SPEC.md`)

- **Form**: the four UIs, told through their demos.
- **Sees and feels**: the name on the first frame, then each UI on its own colour with its theme
  colours, style and demo in use, a cursor leaving a trail, wipes between UIs.
- **First two seconds**: 「my-ui」 and the four names, with the four demos flying in.
- **Sound**: `scripts/soundtrack.ts`, 120 BPM funk / city pop: electric-piano stabs, slap-style
  bass, swung hats, brass hits on the cuts.
